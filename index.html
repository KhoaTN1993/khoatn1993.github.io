FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start training.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. GymFlow has no server and doesn't sync your training data anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What if I change phones?

Export a backup file from Settings, move it to the new phone and restore it. Premium comes back with Restore purchases on the same Google account.

Is there an iPhone version?

GymFlow is on Android for now. An iOS version is planned.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
